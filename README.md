<details>
<summary>🧬 &nbsp;<b>Research Highlights</b></summary>
<br/>

**📊 Hybrid RNN Feature Composition Study**
> Multi-run stability analysis of 5 RNN architectures for stock prediction across 20 independent runs. Demonstrated that feature design (OHLCV + FinBERT sentiment + Technical Indicators) influences model stability more than architectural complexity.

---

**🗜️ CLIP Embedding Compression for RAG**
> First systematic study on how vector compression affects downstream generation quality in multimodal RAG. Benchmarked 12 compression methods on MS-COCO & Flickr30k. Introduced **ERU** (Embedding Retrieval Utility) metric — found retrieval metrics overstate generation loss by up to **65×**.

---

**🕸️ GC-MARL for IoT Self-Healing**
> Proposed GC-MARL combining graph spectral reward shaping, Byzantine robustness, and decentralized policy consensus. Achieved **50% faster** algebraic connectivity recovery, **39% lower** topology oscillation, and **82% Byzantine resilience** on 30-node IoT networks.

---

**📉 Regime-Adaptive RL for Equity Trading**
> Benchmarked PPO, Recurrent PPO, MAML, and Context-Conditioned agents across bull/bear/sideways regimes using forced regime-shift benchmarks. Found simple PPO achieves strongest overall Sharpe; context-conditioned policy best preserves bear-market performance.

</details>
